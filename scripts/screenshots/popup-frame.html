Counts only the videos in Japanese

Inputly works out the language of each video from its audio track, its captions and its title, and counts only the ones in the language you learn.

Wrong guess? One click corrects it, and it is remembered.

Your day ends at 4:00, so a late night counts for the day before.

Netflix and Crunchyroll count too

Turn them on in the settings, and Inputly reads which audio track the player plays: an episode counts only while its audio is in the language you learn.

Switch the audio to another language and the count stops.

YouTube counts as soon as you install it, video by video.
