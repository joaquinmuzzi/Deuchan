# Deuchan

**Counts the time you spend watching German on YouTube, on its own and on your computer.**

[![Tests](https://github.com/joaquinmuzzi/deuchan/actions/workflows/tests.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/joaquinmuzzi/deuchan/actions/workflows/tests.yml)
[![License: MIT](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-blue.svg)](LICENSE)

Deuchan is a browser extension for people who learn German by immersion. It adds up the time you
watch German videos on YouTube, without timers to start or stop, and shows it by day and by week
so you can see how much input you really get.

## Why Deuchan?

- **Automatic.** Pausing, rewinding, looping and changing the speed are handled; it only counts
  while the video plays.
- **Only German.** It works out the language of each video and ignores the rest. You can correct
  it for a video when it is wrong.
- **Local and private.** The log stays in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
